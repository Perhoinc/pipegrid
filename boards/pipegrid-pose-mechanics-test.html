1. ROTATION MATH — GENERATED, NOT HAND-TYPED
All 24 orientation-preserving rotations of a cube, built by repeatedly combining two basic moves (spin around vertical, tip forward) until nothing new turns up. If this table isn't all green, nothing downstream can be trusted.
2. DISTINCT-POSE COUNTS — SHAPE + ROLE, CHECKED AGAINST HAND-DERIVED NUMBERS
Same shape can have a different count depending on whether a flow role (in/out) is assigned. Assigning a role raises the count, never lowers it: a role removes symmetry, and fewer self-matching rotations means more orientations are genuinely tellable apart. A directional straight has 6 poses where a bare one has 3, because the 180° flip that's invisible on the bare shape swaps inlet for outlet once the ends have roles. The roled count is always a whole multiple of the bare count, capped at 24. Bare rows have no flow role at all (silhouette only, never actually placed on the board) — they exist purely to sanity-check the rotation math against a plain shape. The rest are real, placeable flow topologies.
