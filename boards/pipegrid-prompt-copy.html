WHAT IS LOCKED

A tile across the top centre. Amber when the app wants something from you, red when it refused, quiet with a blue edge while you are in the middle of something. No cancel on the tile — you leave an action the way you came in. The corner readout stays, carrying the camera and nothing else.

The tile is not capped and will sit over the camera chip or the compass when a sentence is long — your call, on the grounds that a prompt wants attention and then goes away, while those two can wait their turn. Measured on this page: at a comfortable width none of the seventeen overlap anything; narrow the window to a phone in landscape and fifteen cover the camera chip and one reaches the compass. None run off the edge of the stage. So the overlap is a narrow-window event, not the normal case — make the window small and judge whether it bothers you there.

Every tile below is a real state the app can be in. Where I have shortened the wording, today’s version is printed underneath so you can say which reads better. Nothing here is in the app yet.

ONE GAP THIS TURNED UP

Dragging a group into something solid just stops. Nothing is said, because the drag refuses silently and the group stays where it last fitted. In a crowded stack that is indistinguishable from the drag having gone wrong.

Should a blocked drag say so — a red tile naming the cell in the way, the moment the group stops following your finger?
