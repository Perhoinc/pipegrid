proving the connect/gizmo loop generalizes past one hard-coded shape, before scaling to the full catalogue
Tap any ring on the seed corner to place a new corner-split connected to it, same as step 2. The three coloured rings that appear on the new part are the gizmo, ported straight from pipegrid-pose-gizmo-mockups.html: drag a ring to turn the part — it follows your finger's angle, an amber dashed ghost shows where it'll land, and it snaps to the nearest quarter turn on release. Drag a diamond handle to shift the part a whole cell. This is the control that reaches the other 3 valid poses step 2 couldn't. Drag empty space to orbit. Tap an open port while the gizmo is up and it just dismisses — that tap doesn't also place a part — then tap it again normally to keep the chain going. To bring a part's gizmo back, tap anywhere along its drawn pipe, or tap one of its ports that's already connected — either reselects it directly, no dismiss step needed first. Pick CORNER or ELBOW above to choose what the next tap-to-connect placement is — this is the step proving the connection/pose logic generalizes past a single hard-coded shape, on the way to the full catalogue.
Axis convention note: the existing camera rig was built Y-up (Y is vertical, X/Z are the ground plane). Everything built this session — the pose system, the gizmo, the 2D sheet — is Z-up (U/D are literally ±Z). Adapted the camera here to Z-up rather than the other way around, since the pose system is the more deeply validated of the two. Flagging this as a real decision, not a silent change.
