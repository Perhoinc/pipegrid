step 1 of 4: real pose data + a real corner part, inside the proven orbit camera
This is the first thing in the whole parts-catalogue/pose effort that combines real 24-rotation pose data, real channel geometry (drawn from the construction rule, same as every other board), and the proven orbit/pan/zoom camera from pipegrid-3d-combined-test.html — in one file, for the first time. Nothing here is interactive yet beyond the camera; placement, connection-driven pose, and the gizmo override are steps 2–4. This step is just: does a real posed part render correctly inside the real camera rig.
Axis convention note: the existing camera rig was built Y-up (Y is vertical, X/Z are the ground plane). Everything built this session — the pose system, the gizmo, the 2D sheet — is Z-up (U/D are literally ±Z). Adapted the camera here to Z-up rather than the other way around, since the pose system is the more deeply validated of the two. Flagging this as a real decision, not a silent change.
